---
module-name: digital-site-notice
title: Digital Site Notice
created-by: Camden digital planning team
description: A summary for public display, presenting key details of the proposed development.
applies-if:
  planning-authority: "local-authority:CMD"
fields:
  - field: name-of-development
    description: The name of the proposed development or scheme
  - field: max-height-storeys
    description: Maximum height of the development in storeys
  - field: construction-period
    description: Estimated construction period (start year to end year)
    dataset: construction-period
  - field: proposed-land-uses
    description: Description of proposed land uses
  - field: open-space-m2
    description: Total area of open space to be provided (in square metres)
  - field: total-new-homes
    description: Total number of new homes proposed
  - field: affordable-housing-percentage
    description: Percentage of the total homes that will be affordable housing
  - field: onsite-co2-reduction-percentage
    description: Percentage reduction in on-site CO2 emissions beyond Building Regulations
  - field: access-description
    description: Summary of pedestrian and vehicle access arrangements
  - field: estimated-new-jobs
    description: Estimated number of new jobs the development will create
----
